Experimental model card: endpoint-v1

Status: fitted and reproducible; quality gate FAILED; app default unchanged.
Domain: auxiliary speech-to-EEG ALICE benchmark, not educational video.
Architecture: frozen content/TRIBE representations; last-valid-state residual
TCN EEG readout. Same training folds, scales, targets and three-seed schedule
as cohort-v1. Native pretrained TRIBE fMRI output is unchanged.

Training: S01/S03/S04/S05, clips 1–8. Development: S06, clips 9–10.
Evaluation: S08/S10, clips 11–12, previously observed and now reused.
All results are exploratory, not fresh independent validation.
Output: 40 electrode voltage predictions on a 50 Hz grid for the existing
0.5–8 Hz filtered target. No attention, learning or demographic inference.

A fixed development-only rule selected 20% full endpoint network and
80% acoustic ridge. The TRIBE-only calibration selected zero neural weight.
The uncalibrated full network is also reported, so baseline gains cannot
be mistaken for a standalone multimodal success.

Results (normalized MSE; mean signed Pearson correlation)
previous_full_tcn: 0.949433; -0.07319539705592902
full_endpoint: 0.906187; 0.0074265263162746255
full_calibrated: 0.789026; 0.02903374122691794
previous_acoustic_ridge: 0.785250; 0.03698184694262458
previous_mean: 0.776981; None

The calibrated model reduces error versus the old full model but does not
beat the acoustic or training-mean baselines. One clip remains negatively
correlated. Matching content beats wrong content on both test clips, which
is encouraging but insufficient to establish useful generalization.

Training-person pairwise EEG repeatability in the existing diagnostic was
about r=0.009. That suggests weak shared targets; it is not a validated
noise ceiling or proof that the recordings cannot support another task.
Development calibration gains did not transfer over the acoustic baseline.

Files: config.json, development-selection.json, forecast-lock.json,
results.json, audit.json, example-audit.json, and six numeric model.npz
files under tribe/ and full/. Use speech_eeg.endpoint.load_endpoint;
loading these weights into the original mean-pooling class is incorrect.

Next scientific step: prespecify evaluation on a new story and new people,
and establish training-only target repeatability before expanding model
capacity. Do not choose channels, signs or endpoints to improve this reused
test. Classroom benefit and measured-fMRI superiority remain untested.
